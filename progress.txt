## Codebase Patterns
- Build command: `nim c --mm:orc -o:<out> src/donkey.nim` (config.nims sets styleCheck/warnings/panics globally)
- Test command: `testament --megatest:off all`
- Adapters live in `src/donkey/adapters/channel/` with `_pure.nim` parsers in `src/donkey/domain/channels/`
- `OutboundChannelPorts` in `ports/channel.nim` must list every `ChannelKind`; add new channels here + in `outbound_builder.nim` + in `outbound_runtime.nim` case statements
- CLI channel (`ckCli`) needs: `types.nim` enum entry + `$` case, `OutboundChannelPorts` field, `buildOutboundPorts` wiring, `DaemonAdapters` field + init, `outbound_runtime.nim` cases

  - Adding parameters to `setupIpcServer` required cascading updates: `setupDaemonRuntime` → `activateDaemonBootstrap` → `startDaemon` to pass `memory` through
